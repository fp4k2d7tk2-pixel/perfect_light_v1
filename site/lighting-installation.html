Your Fixtures, or Ours

Flexible on the fixture, not on the installation.

Perfect Light can install customer-selected fixtures, help refine fixture and placement decisions, recommend dimming and color temperature, review control compatibility, or develop a complete lighting plan before installation begins.

What's considered before every installation:

Finished surfaces, ceiling conditions, electrical access and existing wiring, switch and control locations, dimming and LED compatibility, brightness, fixture scale and support, ceiling boxes, and permit requirements.

Drywall repair, plaster repair, painting, and finish carpentry are not assumed to be included and will be confirmed in writing before work begins.
