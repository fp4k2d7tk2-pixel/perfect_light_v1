Chandelier & Pendant Installation in Chicago

A well-hung pendant or chandelier finishes a room. A poorly hung one draws every eye for the wrong reason. Perfect Light handles the box, the support, the hanging height, and the wiring so the fixture looks the way it was intended to.

What Goes Into a Clean Install

The parts most people don't think about.

Most fixture installs look easy from the outside. The parts that matter — a properly rated ceiling box, correct support for the weight, chain or rod length, hanging height above a table or island, and matched dimming — are settled before the fixture ever leaves its box.

Common Locations

Where these fixtures typically go.

Dining rooms

A chandelier hung 30–36 inches above the table, sized to the table's shape, centered properly, and on a matching dimmer.

Kitchen islands

Two or three pendants, evenly spaced, hung high enough to see across the counter but low enough to read as fixtures.

Entryways & foyers

A larger fixture that reads from the door, hung with enough clearance and properly centered on the space.

Stairwells

Long chain or rod, weight support planned for the ceiling condition, and dimming that works from both floors.

Primary bedrooms

Central pendant or small chandelier as an alternative to a ceiling fan, often paired with sconces.

Powder rooms & nooks

Smaller decorative fixtures where a jewel-box moment adds character.

Fixture Support

Heavy fixture? That's a wiring and structural question, not just a purchase.

Heavier chandeliers require ceiling boxes rated for the weight, and older Chicago homes often need the box swapped or reinforced before installation. If a ceiling medallion or additional support is needed, that gets identified and planned before the fixture is hung.

Ceiling fan retrofits with lighting are handled similarly — the box gets confirmed as fan-rated, not just fixture-rated.

A short checklist before install day:

  • Fixture on-site and unpackaged, with all parts confirmed.
  • Ceiling condition and existing box reviewed.
  • Chain, rod, or cord length trimmed to the correct hanging height.
  • Dimmer and LED compatibility verified.
  • Surfaces protected and cleanup handled at the end.
