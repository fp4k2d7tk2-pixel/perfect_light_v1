In Cosimo's own words

Why I started Perfect Light.

I got interested in lighting the same way most people do — walking into a room and realizing something about it just worked. The finish, the furniture, the paint color were all fine. What was doing the work was the light.

Once I started looking at lighting that way, I couldn't stop seeing where it was getting missed. Beautiful kitchens with harsh cans in the wrong places. Great living rooms lit like offices. Dimmers that only go from bright to off. Fixtures that were expensive on their own and wrong for the space.

Most of that isn't the homeowner's fault. It's what happens when the person planning the lighting isn't the same person installing it, and the person installing it is thinking about wire runs and code, not about how the room is going to feel when the sun goes down.

I built Perfect Light so the person who plans the lighting is the person who installs it. Same eye, same hands, same standard from the first walkthrough to the last dimmer setting.
Cosimo Cortese · Owner

Before starting the business I served seven years in the Marine Corps and completed my degree at Penn State. Both taught me the same thing: show up prepared, do the work correctly, and leave the space better than you found it. That is still the standard on every job.

Perfect Light is small on purpose. You reach me directly. I do the walkthrough. I write the plan. I do the install. And I stay accountable for how it turns out.
