Lighting Design Consultation

Not sure what the room needs?

A lighting design consultation helps turn a general idea into a practical plan. Cosimo can review the room, discuss how it is used, identify lighting limitations, and recommend fixture types, placement, brightness, color temperature, dimming, and installation options.

For straightforward work, the consultation may be incorporated into the estimate process. Larger or more design-intensive projects may require a separate paid design engagement. Any fee will be explained before work begins.

Meet the Owner

Meet Cosimo Cortese.

Cosimo Cortese founded Perfect Light Chicago to offer homeowners a more thoughtful and professional approach to lighting and electrical work.

Before entering the electrical trade, Cosimo served seven years in the United States Marine Corps and completed his undergraduate education at Penn State. He later completed his electrical training and apprenticeship and earned both his Supervising Electrician License and Electrical Contractor License.

Perfect Light reflects the standards Cosimo brings to the work: preparation, personal accountability, attention to detail, clear communication, and respect for the customer's home.

Every project is an opportunity to build the company's reputation through work that looks right, functions properly, and earns the next recommendation.
