FAQ

Straight Answers to Common Questions

The questions homeowners ask most often about lighting design, installation, dimmers, and hiring an electrician in Chicago. If your question isn't here, call or email — Cosimo will answer directly.

What areas of Chicago does Perfect Light serve?

Perfect Light Chicago serves homeowners and businesses in Edgewater, Uptown, Lakeview, Wrigleyville, Northalsted, Lincoln Park, Roscoe Village, Lincoln Square, West Town, Ukrainian Village, Hyde Park, and Oak Park. Service availability depends on project type, scheduling, and location. See the full service area.

Is Perfect Light Chicago licensed and insured?

Yes. Perfect Light Chicago is owned and operated by Cosimo Cortese, a licensed Supervising Electrician and Electrical Contractor. The business is insured and bonded. Proof of insurance is available on request.

Do you offer free estimates?

Free estimates are available for straightforward, well-defined projects — fixture installations, dimmer swaps, or single-room recessed lighting, for example. Projects that require design planning (multiple rooms, fixture placement, layered lighting, or whole-home strategy) are handled as a Lighting Design Consultation. Any applicable design fee is explained in advance.

What is a lighting design consultation?

A working session where Cosimo reviews the room, discusses how it's actually used, identifies lighting limitations, and recommends fixture types, placement, brightness, color temperature, dimming, and installation options. For smaller projects, the consultation may fold into the estimate process. Larger or design-intensive projects may involve a separate paid design engagement. More about lighting design →

Can Perfect Light install fixtures I already bought?

Yes. Perfect Light regularly installs customer-selected fixtures. If a fixture has weight, support, or dimming-compatibility issues, Cosimo will identify them before installation begins — not after.

Why do my LED lights flicker or buzz when dimmed?

Almost always a dimmer/LED compatibility problem, not a broken fixture. A matched LED-compatible dimmer paired to the fixture's driver typically resolves it. More on dimmers and smart controls →

How high should a chandelier hang above a dining table?

Typically 30 to 36 inches from the top of the table to the bottom of the fixture, adjusted for ceiling height, fixture size, and how the table is used. The right height is confirmed on-site before final installation. More on chandelier & pendant installs →

How many recessed lights do I need in a room?

There's no fixed number. It depends on room size, ceiling height, how the room is used, beam angle, and whether recessed lights are the primary light or one layer of a larger plan. Perfect Light designs the layout for the specific space rather than a generic spacing rule. More on recessed lighting →

Do you handle drywall or plaster repair after a recessed lighting install?

Not automatically. Whether Perfect Light handles patching or a finisher does is confirmed in writing before work begins so there are no surprises at the end.

What smart lighting systems do you install?

Lutron Caseta, Lutron RA, and comparable professional-grade systems that use physical wall switches as the primary control, with app and voice access as a secondary option. The priority is a system that works whether or not the internet is up.

Do you offer emergency electrical service?

Available based on scheduling and location. Call (312) 478-6298 to check availability.
