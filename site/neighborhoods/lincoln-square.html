About Lincoln Square

Lighting work built for the homes on these blocks.

Lincoln Square combines a historic commercial core with a strong residential base of vintage bungalows, two-flats, and updated single-family homes.

The building stock is a mix of Chicago bungalows, greystones, and renovated single-family homes, often with original architectural details that benefit from careful lighting choices. Perfect Light approaches every Lincoln Square project with an understanding of the building it lives in — ceiling heights, existing wiring, plaster conditions, and the way each room is actually used.
