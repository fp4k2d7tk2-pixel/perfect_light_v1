Lighting Design

Residential Lighting Design in Chicago

Lighting affects how a room looks, how people move through it, and how comfortable it feels at different times of day. Perfect Light Chicago helps homeowners plan lighting that supports the architecture, furniture, daily routines, and desired atmosphere of the space.

The recommendations are developed by Cosimo Cortese, a licensed electrical contractor who can carry the plan through installation — helping avoid the gap between a design concept and what can practically, safely, and cleanly be built.

The Foundation

Three layers of light.

Most rooms that feel right are using all three layers together, not relying on a single overhead source.

Ambient lighting

The general light that establishes the room's overall brightness and mood.

Task lighting

Focused light for cooking, reading, grooming, working, or other specific activities.

Accent lighting

Lighting used to highlight artwork, architectural elements, shelving, textures, or visual focal points.

How It Works

The consultation process.

01

Discovery

Discuss the room, current lighting, style, problems, budget, timing, and desired result.

02

On-site review

Evaluate dimensions, ceiling conditions, natural light, furniture placement, electrical access, existing circuits, controls, and finished surfaces.

03

Recommendations

Develop practical recommendations for fixture types, locations, lighting layers, brightness, color temperature, dimming, and controls.

04

Scope and pricing

Separate design recommendations, fixtures, electrical work, permit costs, and installation costs clearly.

05

Installation

Complete approved work with proper preparation, property protection, testing, cleanup, and communication.

06

Final adjustment

Review dimming levels, aiming, control settings, color temperature, and the finished effect with the customer.
