How We Work

A clear process, from first walkthrough to final dimmer setting.

Perfect Light projects follow a repeatable process built around preparation, communication, and clean execution. Below is what to expect when you hire Cosimo — and what he expects of himself on every job.

01

Discovery Call

A short conversation to understand the project. What rooms are involved. What is not working about the current lighting. What is being renovated or already finished. Whether an interior designer, architect, or general contractor is already involved. Budget range if it is helpful.

15–20 minutes · No cost

02

On-Site Walkthrough

Cosimo visits the home. He looks at the room in person — ceiling height, natural light, existing wiring, sightlines, furniture layout, and how the space is actually used at different times of day. He asks about atmosphere, color temperature preferences, and any specific fixtures already under consideration. Existing electrical is checked while he is there.

45–90 minutes · Scheduled at your convenience

03

Lighting Plan & Estimate

A written plan covering fixture placement, ambient/task/accent layers, color temperature, dimming and controls, and any electrical work required to support it. Delivered with a clear estimate broken out by scope. Revisions are part of the process — the plan is not final until it fits the room, the budget, and the way you want the space to feel.

3–5 business days · Fixed-fee for larger designs, folded into the estimate for straightforward work

04

Fixture Selection

Cosimo can source fixtures directly, work from a designer's specification, or install what you have already purchased. When sourcing, he selects for quality of light first, longevity second, and price third — and explains the trade-offs. Lead times and availability are confirmed before installation is scheduled.

Coordinated around fixture lead times

05

Installation

Cosimo does the installation personally. Drop cloths down before tools come out. Walls, ceilings, and floors are protected. Cuts are precise. Dust is contained. The work area is clean at the end of each day. If any part of the plan needs to change during install — because of a framing surprise, an existing wiring condition, or a change of mind — you hear about it directly, with options and cost impact.

Timeline shared upfront · Progress updates throughout

06

Commissioning & Walkthrough

The lighting is tuned in the space it lives in. Dimmer levels set. Scenes programmed if smart controls are involved. Color temperature and beam angles verified in the actual room, at the actual time of day it matters. Cosimo walks the finished install with you, explains any controls, and confirms nothing on the punch list is left open.

Final visit before invoice closes

07

After the Job

You reach Cosimo directly — not a call center. If anything is not working the way it should, he comes back and makes it right. Warranty on workmanship stands. Fixture warranties are handled through the manufacturer with his help.

Ongoing · Direct line to Cosimo

What doesn't happen

Things you will not experience on a Perfect Light job.

No salesperson handoff

The person who quoted the job is the person who does the job. You never get passed to an installer you have never met.

No surprise pricing

If scope changes mid-job, you hear about it before work continues. No line items appearing on the final invoice.

No mess left behind

Job sites are cleaned daily. Final walkthrough is done with the home as clean as it started, minus the improvements.

No fixture pushing

Cosimo does not upsell fixtures for margin. The recommendation is the fixture that fits the room and the budget you set.

Have a project in mind?

Share a little about the space and what you would like to change. Cosimo will suggest the appropriate next step.
